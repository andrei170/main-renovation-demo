Insurance Claims — the page most roofers do not write

“Will my insurance cover this?”

The honest answer is that it depends on the cause and the age of the roof, and anyone who answers before looking is guessing. We inspect, document, and explain in plain language what we found and what it usually means.

What we do and do not do

We do: inspect, photograph, write the report, meet your adjuster on site and scope the repair. We do not: promise an outcome, tell you what to say, or file anything on your behalf. That is your claim and your insurer's decision.

Repair or replace

We quote both where both are genuinely options, and show you the difference in writing.

After the work

Final photographs, the warranty paperwork, and a number that still answers — we are not leaving the county.

Commercial Roofing — Conklin Systems

Spray polyurethane foam

Seamless, insulating, and installed over many existing roofs without a full tear-off — which keeps the building operating while the work happens.

Roof coatings

Restoration rather than replacement where the deck is sound. Extends service life at a fraction of a tear-off.

Single-ply membrane

TPO and equivalent systems for flat and low-slope commercial roofs, specified to the building rather than to a catalogue.
